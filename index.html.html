About The Project

This project analyzes why petrol price is different in every country even though crude oil is same globally. Using Tableau, we visualized:

  • Global Crude Oil Price Trend (2020-2026)
  • Country-wise Retail Price Comparison
  • Tax Share % vs Base Price
  • Three Taxation Models: High Tax, Subsidy, and De-regulated

Key Insights from Tableau

1. Tax is the Decider: UK price $1.92 vs USA $0.95 - same crude, different tax.


2. Europe = High Tax: 60% price is tax for carbon control & govt revenue.


3. Middle East = Subsidy: UAE & Iran keep prices low by paying from govt budget.
